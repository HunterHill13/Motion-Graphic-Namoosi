# Anti-Cliché Design Principles & Conceptual Semiotics

> Synthesized from `anthropics/skills/frontend-design` and `canvas-design` to eradicate AI-generated visual tropes from Remotion motion pictures.

---

## 1. The Core AI Visual Cliché Tells (Strictly Banned)

AI-generated UI and motion graphics currently cluster around predictable, uninspired tropes. These are strictly forbidden:

1. **The Generic SaaS Card Cluster:** Chopping content into identical rounded rectangular cards with the same `rgba(0,0,0,0.1)` drop shadow and random gradient border.
2. **The Ungrounded ALL-CAPS Eyebrow:** Sticking a tracked-out uppercase label (`OVERVIEW`, `SYSTEM`, `ARCHITECTURE`) above every single headline regardless of context.
3. **The Meaningless Middle Dot:** Joining arbitrary strings with middle dots (`A · B · C`) to simulate complexity.
4. **The Default Dark Mode Acid-Green Glow:** Black canvas paired reflexively with generic toxic green or purple neon lines without subject justification.
5. **Literal Noun Drawing ("The Visual Echo"):** Drawing a literal clock when the voiceover says "time", or drawing a cartoon document when the voiceover says "research".
6. **Emoji Proliferation:** Using Unicode emojis (📄, 💡, 🏆, 🌟, 📊, 🎯) as visual elements. Emojis instantly destroy broadcast credibility.

---

## 2. "Spend Boldness in One Place"

Premier design studios (Buck, Ordinary Folk, Pentagram) operate on the principle of extreme visual discipline:
- **Let One Element Be the Hero:** In every scene, exactly one visual actor or word carries the bold movement or monumental scale.
- **Keep Everything Else Quiet:** Secondary elements, telemetry labels, and background dust remain calm, subtle, and disciplined.
- **Cut Unmotivated Decoration:** If a particle, line, or badge does not clarify spatial relationship or explain physical causality, eliminate it.

---

## 3. Deducing the Subtle Conceptual Metaphor

Instead of literal noun drawing, extract the **physical mechanism** of the concept:
- **Medical Research:** Rather than a cartoon stethoscope, visualize a *laser-caliper measuring molecular bond distances on an isometric plate*.
- **Data Growth:** Rather than a simple bar rising, visualize *stochastic cellular crystallization expanding across an engineered grid*.
- **System Failure:** Rather than a red warning sign, visualize *an aperture iris closing down with directional shutter blur and gravitational singularity compression*.

---

## 4. Grounding in the Subject Matter

1. **Palette Tokens (4 to 6 Hex Values):** Define a cohesive palette derived from real physical materials (e.g. graphite, cold titanium, terracotta ceramic, oxidised copper, deep cobalt).
2. **Semantic Weight:** Visual weight corresponds to real-world significance; large entities possess heavy spring mass ($m \ge 0.8$) and long settle times, while micro-chips possess featherweight mass ($m \le 0.4$) and high stiffness.
